03 / 09
The public-data backbone

City law makes every building legible.

Local Law 84.
Every covered building publicly reports its annual energy use and emissions. That data is the model's primary signal.
Local Law 97.
A carbon cap with penalties starting 2024, tightening sharply in 2030. Buildings under pressure to comply are also the ones most likely to convert off steam.
Plus three more signals
Building permits
HVAC and mechanical work signal electrification in progress.
Deed records
New owners revisit energy contracts within months of acquisition.
Property data
Age, size, use type. The structural facts every other signal sits on top of.

Machine learning

Two questions.
One answer per building.

Question 01
What kind of building is this?
A co-op and a midtown tower disconnect for different reasons. The model needs to know which it's looking at first. We let the data sort all 1,210 buildings into groups before scoring any of them.
k-means clustering
Question 02
How likely is it to disconnect?
12 signals feed in per building. One risk score comes out, along with the top 5 reasons behind it, signed up or down. Not a black box. The caller sees the explanation before picking up the phone.
gradient boosting + SHAP
